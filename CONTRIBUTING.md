# Contributing

Hive BDC is developed and maintained by Balsoft GmbH only. The source is
public for transparency and review; it is not an open-source project.

- **Pull requests** are not accepted and will be closed.
- **Bug reports and feature requests** from licensed customers go through the
  usual Balsoft support channel, not through this repository.
- **Security issues:** please report them privately to security@balsoft.de
  instead of opening anything public.

Thank you for your interest in the product.
